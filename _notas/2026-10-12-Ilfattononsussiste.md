---
title: Il fatto non sussiste
date: 2026-10-12 17:43:30 -0300
---
> “The offence does not exist” (il fatto non sussiste). With those words, Italy’s Corte Suprema di Cassazione has righted a grave injustice done to Fabio De Pasquale and Sergio Spadaro.
> The two Milan prosecutors were charged with withholding evidence that bore on the credibility of a key witness in their bribery prosecution of oil giants ENI and Shell. By failing to make it available to the defence, the charge went, they had committed the crime of refusing to perform official duties.
