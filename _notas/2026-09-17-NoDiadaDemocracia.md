No dia da democracia, em 15 de setembro, no *Verfassungsblog*, "[Indirect Militant Democracy](https://verfassungsblog.de/indirect-militant-democracy/)":

> Indirect militant democracy seeks to make exclusionary politics electorally costly while preserving the freedom to organise and compete. Its rules must be justified by the value of broader support, not by the identity of the party they currently disadvantage. Sonneberg shows why the distinction matters: the AfD won, but its difficulty expanding beyond its initial supporters made the contest closer. Electoral design should make that difficulty matter before democratic defence has to depend on a court excluding a party altogether.
