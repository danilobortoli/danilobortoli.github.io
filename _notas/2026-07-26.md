> The MSG security system runs on a software called Genetec. Guests entering the building typically pass through biometric scanners, after which they can be monitored from camera to camera. Attendees at the Swift-Kelce nuptials would have also bypassed these scanners via a separate, private entrance, per CNN. The memo viewed by Wired confirms that MSG’s outside-facing cameras were left on throughout the multi-day festivities.

Com [isso aqui](https://pitchfork.com/story/madison-square-garden-disabled-its-surveillance-system-for-taylor-swifts-wedding/), lembrei que é possível comprar uma tal de *reasonable expectation of privacy* [afinal](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/25-112_0am4.pdf).
